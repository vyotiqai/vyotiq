# Frontend design

## Instructions
1. Follow the existing design system and patterns before inventing new ones.
2. One clear job per section; avoid dashboard clutter on non-dashboard surfaces.
3. Prefer purposeful typography and atmosphere on new branded surfaces.
4. Keep interactions obvious; do not wrap everything in cards or pills.
5. Ensure desktop and mobile layouts both work; check contrast and spacing.
6. Use motion sparingly for hierarchy and presence, not decoration.
7. Ask 1–2 clarifying questions only when brand direction or audience is unclear.
